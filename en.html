ABOUT

River, light, and a slow cup

Marina Park Cafe sits on the ground floor of Marina Park at Seoraenaru, Banpo Han River Park — a building that seems to float on the river. Floor-to-ceiling windows open onto the Han River and the Seoul skyline, and the terrace under the red awning puts you right in front of the sunset and the city lights.

Coffee, tea and desserts; pizza, pasta and steak; wine, beer and highballs in the evening — a place for a quick stop after a riverside walk, or a long dinner on a special day.

PRIVATE PARTY

Small gatherings by the river

The cafe garden and terrace can be booked for groups of 10–30: birthdays, club gatherings, bridal showers and small year-end parties. Bring a cake and simple decorations — the river sunset does the rest.

Banquets of 50 or more, corporate events and weddings are held in the 2F convention hall (100–300 guests) and the 3F rooftop garden of the same building — Marina Park banquet & wedding.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are the opening hours?

We are open daily 10:00 – 22:00. Brunch sets are served until 16:00. On days with private events, part of the space may be reserved.

Where is the cafe? Is there parking?

On the ground floor of Marina Park at Seoraenaru, Banpo Han River Park (115-1 Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu, Seoul). Use the Banpo Han River Park parking lot — a short walk along the river.

Do you have seats with a river view?

The whole ground floor faces the Han River through floor-to-ceiling windows, and the outdoor terrace under the red awning looks straight onto the river and the Seoul skyline.

Can I book a birthday or a small party?

Yes — the cafe garden and terrace host groups of 10–30 for birthdays, club meetings, bridal showers and small year-end parties. You may bring a cake and simple decorations. Larger events use the 2F convention hall and 3F rooftop garden of Marina Park.

Do you serve meals?

Yes: pizza, pasta, pork cutlet, hamburg steak, chop steak, fried rice and more, plus a brunch set, wine, beer and highballs.
